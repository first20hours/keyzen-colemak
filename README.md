Keyzen - Colemak Edition
========================

This is a fork of [Keyzen by wwwtyro](https://github.com/wwwtyro/keyzen), modified for learning the [Colemak](https://colemak.com/) keyboard layout.

The key sequence focuses on learning the Colemak home row, then gradually introduces keys in the top and bottom rows. Common punctuation and web navigation characters are added to the mix after learning every lowercase letter. Alpha letters and uncommon punctuation marks are added last.

Keyzen automatically adds new characters to the mix as your errors decrease. Click on the character sequence at the top to increase or decrease difficulty.

Most modern operating systems have the Colemak keyboard set installed by default. If you're having trouble finding it, Google is your friend. Installation information is also available at [Colemak.com](https://colemak.com/Download).

What's New in This Fork
-----------------------

On top of the original trainer, this fork adds:

- **Colemak reference keyboard.** Click `kb` in the bottom-right corner, or press Esc, to show a small Colemak layout. The next key to type is highlighted, and both Shift keys light up for capitals and symbols. The home row is darker, and T and N have small bumps like real home keys.
- **Most-missed keys.** Click `stats` in the bottom-left corner to see the 10 keys you miss most. Each row shows how often you missed the key, your accuracy on it, and the key you usually press instead (`→ x`). Use `reset` to clear the list.
- **Focus weak keys (optional).** In the stats panel, turn on `focus weak keys` to see your most-missed keys a bit more often in practice words. It's off by default, so the trainer works exactly like the original unless you turn it on.
- **No-mistake streak.** The counter at the bottom center shows how many keys you've typed correctly since your last mistake, with your best run next to it.

Your stats, streak and settings are saved in your browser, so they're still there next time.
